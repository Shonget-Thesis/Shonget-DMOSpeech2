## 1. Executive Summary

During the 240-run diagnostic sweep of the fixed handover timing problem, **Instance 44** produced an extreme statistical outlier: a reported Word Error Rate of **2775.0%** under the default $t_{\text{switch}}=0.25$ baseline.

This audit determines whether this metric represented:
1. Repeated or looping speech generated by the synthesis model;
2. An ASR transcription hallucination loop;
3. Corrupted reference text;
4. Or a genuine acoustic failure of the baseline.

**Finding:** The 2775% WER is a **Whisper ASR decoder hallucination loop** triggered by acoustic noise artifacts produced when switching at $t=0.25$ under a low-SNR prompt (15.0 dB). The synthesized audio was only 2.67 seconds long, but Whisper transcribed an infinite YouTube outro loop (*"Like, Subscribe, Like, Share..."*) repeated 111 times. Crucially, holding teacher steps longer until **$t=0.40$ completely eliminated the artifact**, recovering perfect pronunciation with **0.0% WER**.

## 4. Technical Root Cause Analysis

1. **Why did $t=0.25$ produce 2775% WER?**  
   Under a noisy reference prompt ($\text{SNR}=15\text{ dB}$), the intermediate flow trajectory at $t=0.25$ is corrupted by residual ambient noise. The few-step student generator (steps 1 and 2) fails to denoise the acoustic trajectory and outputs low-energy high-frequency static. 
   When OpenAI Whisper (`whisper-base`) transcribes low-energy non-speech static or repetitive noise, its cross-attention autoregressive decoder collapses into well-documented training priors (YouTube metadata/outro tokens: `"Like, Subscribe, Share"`). Because the target text is only 8 words, a 222-word hallucinated transcript produces an insertion penalty of $\frac{222}{8} = 27.75$ ($\mathbf{2775.0\% \text{ WER}}$).

2. **Why does $t=0.40$ fix the problem?**  
   At $t=0.40$, the teacher model runs for 6 steps, allowing the continuous flow-matching ODE to overcome the 15 dB prompt noise and establish stable formant trajectories. When the student takes over at $t=0.40$, it receives a well-formed latent state, producing crisp, intelligible speech with **0.0% WER** and **0.8124 speaker similarity**.

## 5. Methodological Decisions for the Thesis

To prevent this mathematical artifact from distorting statistical analyses while preserving data integrity:

1. **Dual Metric Reporting:**
   * **Report Median WER:** The median for policy $t=0.25$ across the 48 instances is **0.0%**, which is completely immune to this outlier.
   * **Report Capped Mean WER:** To prevent an infinite insertion penalty from an ASR hallucination loop from skewing arithmetic averages, per-utterance WER is capped at $100.0\%$ for mean calculations, alongside the raw uncapped mean.
   
2. **Dual Auditing Standards:**
   * **Reference-Relative Non-Inferiority:** $\text{WER} \le \text{ref\_wer} + 1.0\%$.
   * **Strict Absolute Intelligibility Ceiling:** $\text{WER} \le 20.0\%$ and $\text{SIM} \ge 0.70$.
   * Because Instance 44 had a defective baseline, matching the baseline at $t=0.25$ satisfies relative non-inferiority but fails the absolute quality ceiling. This proves the thesis insight: **under severe noise, safe scheduling requires dynamically upgrading to $t=0.40$.**
